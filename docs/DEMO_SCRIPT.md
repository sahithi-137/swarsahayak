# SwarSahayak (स्वर सहायक) — Live Demonstration & Video Script

**Target Duration**: 4–5 Minutes  
**Demonstrator**: Full-Stack Presenter  
**Prerequisites**:
- Frontend running at `http://127.0.0.1:5173`
- Backend running at `http://127.0.0.1:8000` (Status pill in navbar shows "Online")
- Microphone enabled on Chrome or Edge browser.

---

## Scene 1: Introduction & Homepage Overview (0:00 – 0:45)
**Visual**: Screen shows SwarSahayak homepage with Forest Green theme, clean logo, emergency helpline ribbon, giant mic button, and 4 service cards.

**Voiceover / Action**:
> "Namaste and welcome. Today we are demonstrating **SwarSahayak** (स्वर सहायक), a multilingual voice-based digital assistant designed specifically for rural and tribal citizens across India.
> Millions of citizens in our villages struggle with complex digital menus, dense English text, and keyboard typing. SwarSahayak transforms public service access into a simple, voice-first conversation in their native language."
*(Action: Point to the emergency ribbon showing 108 Ambulance, 1930 Cyber Fraud, and 102 Mother & Child).*

---

## Scene 2: Voice Question in Hindi — Government Schemes (0:45 – 1:30)
**Visual**: User clicks the giant microphone button.

**Voiceover / Action**:
> "Let's first ask a question in Hindi about farming income support. I press the microphone button."
*(Action: Click the microphone button. The button glows crimson, pulse rings animate, and the live status shows 'सुन रहे हैं... कृपया बोलें' (Listening...)).*
*(Spoken Input)*: *"प्रधानमंत्री किसान सम्मान निधि योजना के ₹6,000 कैसे मिलेंगे?"*
*(Visual)*: The recognized Hindi text appears in real-time in the query box. The user clicks **पूछें (Ask)**.
*(Visual)*: In under 0.5 seconds, the verified answer card slides in smoothly.
*(Action: Click the 'उत्तर सुनें' (Listen Answer) button. The audio speaks clearly in Hindi).*
> "Notice the clear structure: eligibility criteria, required documents (Aadhaar, Khata/Khatian, bank passbook), and the verified official link to **pmkisan.gov.in** with an audited verification timestamp."

---

## Scene 3: Safe Banking — Fraud Prevention Alert (1:30 – 2:15)
**Visual**: User clicks the **सुरक्षित बैंकिंग (Safe Banking)** card.

**Voiceover / Action**:
> "Next, digital banking fraud is a severe problem in rural India. Middlemen and scam callers trick villagers into sending money. Let's see how SwarSahayak educates citizens."
*(Action: Click 'Ask Verified Info' on the Safe Banking card).*
*(Visual)*: Answer displays the Golden Rules:
> "1. UPI PIN is ONLY for sending money, never for receiving money.
> 2. Bank officials and police never ask for OTP or CVV.
> 3. If defrauded, dial **1930** immediately within the golden hour to freeze funds."
*(Voiceover)*: "Notice our permanent safety disclaimer: *SwarSahayak NEVER asks for your UPI PIN, OTP, or passwords.*"

---

## Scene 4: Regional (Bhojpuri) & Tribal Linguistic Honesty (2:15 – 3:30)
**Visual**: User opens the Language Selector modal.

**Voiceover / Action**:
> "Now let us examine one of our greatest engineering innovations: our multilingual language architecture. We support 17 languages, including regional languages like **Bhojpuri (भोजपुरी)** and 4 indigenous tribal languages."
*(Action: Open language selector, click the 'Regional (Bhojpuri)' tab).*
> "Notice how Bhojpuri is classified with its own dedicated badge: 'Text & Curated Answers (Voice in Research Pilot)'. Most assistants either omit Bhojpuri or falsely force a Hindi speech engine that mangles authentic grammar. SwarSahayak refuses to do this."
*(Action: Select Bhojpuri (भोजपुरी)).*
*(Visual)*: The entire UI switches to authentic Bhojpuri labels (*"सवाल पूछीं", "स्वर सहायक", "सत्यापित उत्तर"*).
*(Action: Type or click the Bhojpuri prompt: "हमार पीएम किसान के ₹2000 कइया ले आई?")*.
*(Visual)*: The answer card renders in authentic Bhojpuri phrasing (*"हर चार महीना पर ₹2,000-₹2,000 कइके सीधे बैंक खाता में भेजल जाला... जमीन के खतियान/रसीद"*). The audio badge states:
*"भोजपुरी में लिखित जानकारी उपलब्ध बा। प्रामाणिक उच्चारण सुरक्षित रखे खातिर मैनुअल रीडिंग मोड चालू बा।"*
> "Notice how our engine never converts the query or answer into Hindi. Authentic vocabulary (*बा*, *पइसा*, *रउआ*) is strictly preserved, and Manual Reading Mode is activated transparently."
*(Action: Click 'Tribal Languages (4)' tab and showcase Santali (Ol Chiki script)).*

## Scene 5: Simplified Mode (सरल मोड) for Elders (3:30 – 4:15)
**Visual**: User toggles the **सरल मोड (Easy Mode)** button in the navbar.

**Voiceover / Action**:
> "For elderly villagers or citizens with very limited digital literacy, we built **Simplified Mode**."
*(Action: Click 'सरल मोड').*
*(Visual)*:
- The UI transforms into an ultra-high contrast, large-type interface.
- The microphone button expands to 130px.
- Service cards enlarge with prominent one-touch action buttons.
- Audio answers auto-play automatically upon question submission.
*(Action: Switch speech speed to 0.8x (धीमा)).*
> "Citizens can adjust speech speed to 0.8x for slow, crystalline pronunciation."
